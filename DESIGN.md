# Finding something to do with a free hour

Roamday is a task-focused application. The first action is choosing an origin and a free window. A returning user can reuse interests and budget without reentering them.

| Entry concept | Desktop | Phone | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Map and shortlist | Time/location controls above a map with three nearby picks underneath | Map above an expandable, keyboard-accessible results sheet with a list-only view | Selected. Compare real places and return times immediately. |
| Chat first | Conversation fills the canvas, with a map opened from a result | Conversation followed by a map tab | Useful for refinement, adds typing before exploration. |
| Calendar first | Pick a day and block time before browsing | Agenda followed by place details | Useful for advance planners, adds steps for spontaneous breaks. |

A numbered pin and its matching place share a number, name and selection state. Every pick has a small timeline for outward travel, visit and return, plus a written return time. Open a place and return to the same list position and map bounds. A rain-aware result explains whether the visit is sheltered and whether travel is exposed.

Use Instrument Sans for controls, a readable serif heading for venue names, warm paper and deep forest tones with amber for weather. Prefer attributable photos of the actual place when licensed. Include animated sun/moon theme toggle with reduced-motion support. Avoid a dashboard, sidebar, decorative chart, promotional hero and repeated tiles of filler statistics. Test map alternatives, daylight contrast, text zoom, keyboard focus and both themes on phone, tablet and desktop. Concrete direction proposed for approval. No screens implemented.
